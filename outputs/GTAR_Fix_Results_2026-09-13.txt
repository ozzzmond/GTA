### TASK 1 [P1 - AUDIT N1]: Preserve Android Setlist Trash Through Sync
- Problem:
  RoomSyncStore.read() exported only active setlists (isDeleted = false), but
  RoomSyncStore.apply() was enumerating all stored setlists in Room and permanently
  deleting local IDs absent from the incoming active-only payload. Soft-deleting
  a setlist and syncing resulted in permanent destruction of the trashed setlist
  and its cross-reference memberships.
- Resolution:
  1. Updated `RoomSyncStore.kt` apply() method:
     - Established safe sync trash semantics: transport omission of soft-deleted
       items does NOT authorize local permanent deletion.
     - Separated incoming active setlists from local trashed setlists.
     - Trashed rows (isDeleted = true) and their memberships survive sync snapshots
       intact and remain restorable from Android trash.
     - Active local setlists omitted from incoming snapshots are soft-deleted
       (moved to trash) rather than purged.
  2. Reserved permanent deletion strictly for explicit user-initiated purge.
  3. Added `getSetlistById(id: Long)` to `SetlistDao.kt` and updated `FakeSetlistDao`
     implementations across the test suite.
  4. Added integration test `preserveAndroidSetlistTrashThroughSyncAndRestore` in
     `DriveSyncTest.kt` verifying:
     - Active + trashed setlists concurrently stored.
     - Applying unchanged active sync snapshot preserves trashed setlists and memberships.
     - Remote edits to active setlists do not alter trashed items.
     - Soft-deleted setlists can be restored from trash with original songs intact.
     - Explicit permanent delete purges rows only when requested by user.

  3. Added peeled tag commit SHA verification (`verify_remote_tag_peeled_sha`):
